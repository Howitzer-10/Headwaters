## Summary
The secret scan now looks at each message's string leaves joined in order (no separator and one space) around every leaf boundary, and at the percent-decoded and base64-decoded forms of each leaf, one level deep. All bounds are `rules.secrets.scan_*` keys. Results still hold pattern name and span only.
