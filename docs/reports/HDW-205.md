## Deviations
- The packet says `headwaters_more` enables tools "via `tools/list_changed`". On the 2026-07-28 protocol a server cannot push that notification unasked (A-MCP-11), and the daemon is shared by several hosts, so the advanced tools are hidden by a per-host filter keyed on `_meta["headwaters/client"]` and the change is announced on a `subscriptions/listen` stream (plus the classic notification for older hosts). A host that never re-lists sees no change until it does.
- Through the shim, scope loosening always returns `confirmation_required`: the shim's connection to the daemon declares no elicitation capability and the shim does not forward elicitation to the host (HDW-204 left that open), so the daemon correctly sees nobody to ask. Direct clients that declare elicitation are asked. This follows D9 (text fallback; nothing changes until the user confirms) but is stricter than the ideal (FU-205-1).
- Small additive changes outside the five tools, needed to meet the ticket: `decision_id` in `do`/`ask` results (for feedback); `strategy` in `rules_edit.EDITABLE_KEYS`; `meta_of`, `classify` and `tool_annotations` made public in `frontdoor.py`.
- `headwaters_job_status` and `_job_result` read the `jobs` table, but nothing creates jobs until HDW-206.
- No conflict with D7 to D14. D12 holds (think is core, read-only, no reasoning text). D8 and D14 hold (SDK imports only inside `headwaters/mcp/`; the daemon imports no SDK).
