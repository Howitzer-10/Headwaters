## Summary
`_load_litellm` now sets `suppress_debug_info=True` and `set_verbose=False` (with `telemetry=False`) and replaces the handlers of the `LiteLLM`, `LiteLLM Router` and `LiteLLM Proxy` loggers with one stderr handler carrying `RedactionFilter`, level capped at WARNING, no propagation. This closes F8: litellm's `print()` and its stdout log handler can no longer corrupt MCP stdio, and keys in litellm log records are masked. A call-time test with real litellm and blocked sockets shows a failing streaming call only tries the provider host (FU-124-2).
