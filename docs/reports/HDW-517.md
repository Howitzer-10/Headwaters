## Summary

The router now optionally performs **one** retry on a strictly lower tier when the verification gate
reports a *mechanical* failure on a draft, and only after the existing escalation path has declined.
The second answer is returned whatever it is, and the exchange is labelled with `cascade_to`
(separate from `escalated_to`) in the outcome row and the ledger event. Off by default: with
`verify_cascade = false` nothing about the existing behaviour moved, including the schema of the
outcome row, which only gained a nullable column.

A cascade may only move toward the cheap end, so the load-time validator now also refuses
`verify_cascade: true` with `tier_direction: local_first`: on that walk the next tier down is
`openrouter_auto` or `paid`, i.e. the cascade would spend money and then record it in `cascade_to`,
whose whole justification is that it is *not* the "spent more" column (D7).

The honest headline: **this fires on malformed drafts, not on unsatisfying ones.** The probe that
matters (P4) says the gate can check anything on only 19 of 100 real-shaped answers and found zero
faults in them, so on prose traffic the cascade will almost never fire. That is the measured answer,
not a prediction, and it is why the capability page says "structural faults only".


What that means, stated plainly:

- The premise "a failed check makes a downgrade safe" is **true but narrow**: it holds for answers
  whose shape can be parsed (JSON against a schema, fenced code, diffs, tool-call arguments). On
  documentation-style prose the gate mostly has nothing to check (19 %), and what it did check
  passed.
- The cascade is therefore close to dead code on chat traffic and useful on structured traffic. It
  is not a quality mechanism and must never be sold as one (D7, D20).
- This corpus is not live traffic. The number is a **false-positive floor and a coverage measure**,
  not a production cascade rate; a live rate can only come from the owner's own traffic (FU-517-1).

The probes do not contradict the packet's premises, so phase 1 is candidate (c) only, as the packet
said. Two packet facts did turn out stale, and both are recorded under *Deviations*: the HDW-513
UNMERGED markers, and the `headwaters/core/openai_routes.py` path.
