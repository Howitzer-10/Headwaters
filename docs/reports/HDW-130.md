## Summary
A mandatory verification gate that checked nothing is no longer a pass (F11). `VerifyReport.checked` and `VerifyOutcome.checked` count checks that really ran (skips excluded). When the decision is `verify_required` and `checked == 0`, the outcome is flagged `unchecked`, the outcome row stores `verify_result="unchecked"`, and `below_floor` stays visible on `RouteResult`.
